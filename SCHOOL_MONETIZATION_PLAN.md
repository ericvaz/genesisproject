# Genesis Project School Monetization Plan

## Core recommendation

Do not make programmatic ads the school monetization model. For schools, the strongest model is:

1. Free student-facing website.
2. Paid teacher pack.
3. Annual school license for supported, branded, offline classroom use.
4. Association, church, homeschool, or curriculum-network licenses.
5. Carefully selected sponsorships only outside the student-facing classroom experience.

## Why this model fits the current project

The project is already a static, classroom-friendly visual study site. Its strengths are the timeline, chapter explorer, KJV reader, family tree, and study prompts. Schools will pay less for access to the public site itself and more for classroom readiness.

The paid product should therefore be the teacher layer around the site:

- lesson plans
- worksheets
- reflection questions
- quizzes
- printable family trees
- slides
- onboarding guide
- offline package
- optional branded school copy

## Recommended offers

### Free Classroom Pilot

Price: free

Purpose: adoption and teacher feedback.

Includes:

- public Genesis Project site
- timeline
- KJV reader
- family tree explorer
- story room resources

### Teacher Pack

Price: CAD $49 one-time

Target buyer: individual Christian school teacher, homeschool parent, youth pastor, Bible study leader.

Includes:

- PDF lesson plans
- worksheet set
- discussion prompts
- chapter quiz templates
- printable lineage maps
- suggested 4-week, 8-week, and 12-week teaching plans

### Annual School Access

Price: CAD $399 per year

Target buyer: small Christian schools.

Includes:

- teacher pack
- offline zip
- school onboarding guide
- minor yearly updates
- optional school-branded copy
- email support

### Association License

Price: custom, suggested CAD $2,500 to CAD $7,500 per year depending on number of schools.

Target buyer: Christian school associations, homeschool networks, church networks, private-school groups.

Includes:

- multi-school access
- co-branded version
- teacher training session
- annual updates
- early access to Exodus and other future modules

## What to build next

### 1. Teacher Pack MVP

Create a downloadable PDF or Google Docs bundle:

- Lesson 1: Creation and order, Genesis 1 to 2
- Lesson 2: Fall and alienation, Genesis 3 to 5
- Lesson 3: Noah and covenant, Genesis 6 to 9
- Lesson 4: Babel and the nations, Genesis 10 to 11
- Lesson 5: Abraham and promise, Genesis 12 to 25
- Lesson 6: Isaac and continuity, Genesis 21 to 27
- Lesson 7: Jacob and Israel, Genesis 25 to 36
- Lesson 8: Joseph and providence, Genesis 37 to 50

Each lesson should include:

- learning objective
- key passage
- visual activity using the site
- five discussion questions
- short written response
- optional assessment prompt

### 2. School landing page

The project now has a stronger school section with:

- free classroom pilot CTA
- teacher pack offer
- annual school access
- association license
- classroom-safe positioning

Replace the placeholder email address with the actual contact email before publishing.

### 3. Distribution channels

Start with warm, focused outreach:

- Christian schools in Ontario
- Christian school associations
- homeschool groups
- churches with youth ministries
- Bible teachers on LinkedIn
- private school networks

Lead message:

"I built a calm, visual Genesis study tool for my own Bible study, and I am opening a free classroom pilot for Christian schools. It has a Genesis timeline, KJV chapter reader, family tree, and story room resources. Would one Bible teacher at your school be willing to test it?"

## What not to do first

- Do not lead with AdSense for schools.
- Do not require student accounts in the first version.
- Do not collect names, ages, emails, or quiz answers from students.
- Do not include copyrighted Bible translations unless licensed.
- Do not make the public site feel commercial before it has teacher trust.

## Best revenue path

The best first revenue is not ads. It is a CAD $49 teacher pack because it has low friction and lets one motivated teacher buy immediately.

The best scalable revenue is the CAD $399 annual school license because it converts a useful classroom tool into institutional adoption.

The best long-term revenue is the association license plus future modules for Exodus, the Gospels, Acts, and Paul.
