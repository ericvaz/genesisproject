# Genesis Project

A static study site for exploring the Book of Genesis through:

- 8 major story movements
- a family tree with drag and zoom
- a full 50-chapter explorer
- an in-page KJV scripture reader
- a dynamic story room with study resources
- quick search across people, chapters, and stories
- a school pilot and licensing section

## Main sections

- Home hero
- The Chapters
- Family Tree
- Full Chapter Explorer
- Story Room
- Schools
- About

## Latest fixes

- Family tree portrait labels now remain visible under profile pictures instead of being clipped by the circular image node.
- The Chapter Explorer includes a scrollable King James Version reader inside the chapter detail panel.
- The project is KJV-only for simplicity and cleaner school distribution.
- The school section now includes a free pilot, teacher pack, annual school access, and association-license offer.
- Classroom positioning emphasizes no student accounts, no student data collection, and no student-facing programmatic ads.
