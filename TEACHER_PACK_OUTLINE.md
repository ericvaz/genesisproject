# Genesis Project Teacher Pack Outline

## Product name

Genesis Project Teacher Pack

## Price

Suggested launch price: CAD $49

## Audience

Christian school teachers, homeschool parents, youth pastors, Bible study leaders, and private school curriculum coordinators.

## Contents

### 1. Teacher guide

- How to use the Genesis Project site
- Suggested age ranges
- Suggested classroom pacing
- Notes on KJV-only scripture use
- No student-login classroom setup

### 2. Eight lesson plans

Each lesson should include:

- objective
- key chapters
- visual site activity
- close reading activity
- discussion questions
- short writing prompt
- optional quiz

### 3. Printable worksheets

- Creation worksheet
- Fall worksheet
- Noah and covenant worksheet
- Babel and nations worksheet
- Abraham and promise worksheet
- Isaac and continuity worksheet
- Jacob and Israel worksheet
- Joseph and providence worksheet

### 4. Printable family trees

- Adam to Seth
- Noah and sons
- Abraham, Sarah, Hagar, Isaac, and Ishmael
- Isaac, Rebekah, Jacob, and Esau
- Jacob, wives, and children
- Joseph in Egypt

### 5. Assessment prompts

- Short response questions
- Timeline reconstruction exercises
- Character relationship exercises
- Compare and reflect prompts

### 6. Optional classroom slides

- 8 short decks, one per major story movement
- Each deck should have 5 to 8 slides
- Keep the same visual style as the site

## Launch note

This can be produced before building any backend. It is the fastest way to turn the project into a sellable school product.
